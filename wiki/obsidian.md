# Obsidian

Obsidian is a Markdown-based knowledge-base app used in the source as the interactive interface for an [[llm-maintained-knowledge-wikis|LLM-maintained wiki]]. The source frames Obsidian as the IDE for the wiki: the human browses pages, follows links, checks the graph view, and reviews the agent's edits in real time.

Several Obsidian features support this workflow:

- Wikilinks make concept and entity pages easy to connect.
- Graph view reveals hubs, orphans, and the shape of the knowledge base.
- Web Clipper can convert web articles into Markdown sources.
- Local attachment handling can preserve images from clipped articles.
- Plugins such as Dataview and Marp can turn structured frontmatter or Markdown decks into richer outputs.

In this vault, Obsidian is the reading and navigation layer, while the LLM maintains the wiki files directly.
